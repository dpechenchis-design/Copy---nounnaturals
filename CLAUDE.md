## What this is
Noun Naturals' AI native creative studio and content system, separate from the Noun ERP (erp.incivera.ai). Two halves sharing one library and brand kit: an AI Creative Studio (generate and edit images, videos, music and carousels with every model we can reach, Higgsfield first) and the content system below. A web app, used mostly on phones, that runs the content calendar for our editing team in Ukraine:
- Weekly content calendar (week, day, type, format, platforms, status), replacing the Notion calendar (import it once with the Notion API).
- Editors upload finished videos straight to storage (never through our server), at full quality.
- We review in the app: comments pinned to a moment in the video, approve or request edits. Statuses: draft, sent for approval, needs edits, approved, scheduled, posted.
- Notices to the team by Gmail (later WhatsApp Business).
- Posting to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, only after the owner approves.
- Post numbers from each platform, to see what worked. Tying posts to ERP sales is on hold (owner, Nov 2026).
- Goal: about 30 different videos a day, so every flow must scale. Many accounts per platform, each shown by its username.
- Approval is a swipe feed; approved videos post automatically and always show who approved them.
- Hooks: many hooks recorded for one video; a multi video maker cuts each hook onto the body for testing.
- Notices go by Telegram (Google Chat as fallback), not an email per item. No Slack. Frame.io only until the one time move (docs/FRAMEIO-IMPORT.md): its links show as Watch chips until each edit is moved in.
- Team: Khaled owner; Steven and David admins; the Ukraine editing team is an agency. Custom roles later. Sign in is Google only (the same nounnaturals.com accounts as the ERP); only the owner and admins invite people. An admin approval is enough to schedule.
- Approval before posting (owner, Nov 14 2026): an option per person, a second box under their posting permissions, "Needs admin approval before posting". Ticked is the default and today's rule for everyone; unticked lets that one person post without an approval. He will change this again soon, so keep it a simple flag.
- Week tables are editable in full view and take imports (owner, Nov 14 2026): add and remove rows (never a hard delete), edit cells in place, the table fills the page on a computer with a compact two row header. An Import pill reads CSV, Excel and pasted tables and matches columns and values by itself (other names, word order, close spelling), always showing the match before it is applied. No import may fail over a small misspelling.
- Posting permissions (owner, Nov 14 2026): one permission per person with the channels listed under it (tick each channel or all channels). No channel ticked means no posting permission; without it Post and Schedule show grayed out. The owner and admins always have it. Every new platform connection (YouTube, Meta ...) is added to that list in the same change.
- The Calendar is spaces first (owner, Nov 14 2026; he calls them "spaces" like Notion, never "collections", on screen or in the code: files, routes and names all say `space`): tiles for the Noun Naturals content space and the Founder content space (more later), each with its own weeks, table (the main view), board, calendar, gallery and to do list. It replaces the content team's Notion, built by Daryna's team. Fixed columns per space (custom columns are parked: docs/CUSTOM-COLUMNS.md). Custom statuses are allowed, each in a stage, colored with the Noun ERP's pill color picker. Access is per person per space. A "Shot" checkbox, ticked on a phone in the field, saves who and when. The edited video plays in the row, never a link. Status follows what happened. Home, Review and Upload are shared across the spaces a person may see. Not built until the owner approves the plan.
- Every status, Type and Format pill is painted (owner, Nov 15 2026): its dot (or a long press on a phone) opens the Noun ERP's color and gradient picker and the choice changes that pill everywhere for the whole team (owner, admins and Strategist/Editor, who also edit the brand kit: owner, Nov 16 2026; keys calendar.setup and brand.manage). Types and formats each have their own color; a new one gets a free color by itself. Platform pills keep their brand colors. When copying an ERP feature, copy how it is reached, not just the component.
- Less scrolling (owner, Nov 15 2026): the box for a comment or direction on a frame opens beside the video (FrameComposer), never below it; Open on a card is a side peek like Notion with "Open full page".
- Roles (owner, Nov 15 2026): four prebuilt roles (Owner, Admin, Strategist/Editor, Viewer: Strategist and Editor merged into one, owner Nov 15 2026) plus custom roles built from one fixed permission list (src/lib/access/permissions.ts equals the permissions table). Every new feature adds its permission key to that list and to the seed in the same change. Code checks permissions, never role names. The owner and admins build roles: every role but Owner is edited by ticking, and a "Create a custom role" box is always on the Roles page. A "See as" dropdown shows the app as any role, on a test run only, never in the published app (owner, Nov 15). Invites are real emails from our Gmail through the Gmail API with our own keys, set up with the Drive API. An Integrations page like the ERP's (Settings) shows every integration with its logo, status and health, with Refresh and Resync (permission integrations.manage); every new connection gets its card there in the same change.
- Directions on raw clips (the most important workflow): after upload we open a raw clip, mark moments and draw on it like Frame.io; the editor can reply with a question. Editor Home shows raw clips with our directions and our notes asking for a new version; owner and admin Home shows new versions and new clips from editors. Reuse the Review note tools. Show the design before building.
- Clean data is the core goal: every raw clip, version, AI variant (what changed, which tool, cost), timestamped comment, approval, post and number is saved as structured data for the future AI agent and AI editor (built later, in their own chat). Fixed label lists, never hard delete, never lock into one AI vendor.
- Higgsfield is the first video AI tool, through its own API key (open.higgsfield.ai, prepaid, server side only, entered by the owner in Replit Secrets). Notion is being phased out (as of Nov 14 2026 it holds Week 1 with 70 rows, Week 2, drafts of Weeks 3 and 4, and a founder content calendar of 21 items; see docs/DECISIONS.md). Replaces Frame.io: every edit is a new version (v1, v2, ...) with its full history.
- Posting starts with TikTok and Instagram (plus Facebook); YouTube a few weeks later.
- AI native: an agent reads our posts and their numbers and suggests what to make next.
